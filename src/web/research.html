What this does—and does not do

Two readers finding the same page count as one document, not two independent confirmations. Host counts do not establish independent ownership. Retrieval time is not the publication time or the freshness of a token price.

This workspace does not submit paper or real orders, score win rates, enable a database, or restart the paused research archive. Existing paper trading stays in the terminal. The research-to-trading engine and outcome evaluator still need their own verified implementation.
